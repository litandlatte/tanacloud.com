About

I'm Rohit Kumar, a Senior Data Scientist in commercial and trading analytics. Fifteen years ago I was writing requirements documents. Since then I've built forecasting engines, optimization models, geospatial systems and generative AI tools—the kind that have to run unattended on a Monday morning, not the kind that survive a single demo.

Most of what I teach here, I had to make work first. The H3 session exists because I replaced a brute-force distance calculation with a hexagonal spatial index in production, and most of the math simply stopped being necessary. That's the only qualification I trust.

So this isn't courseware. These are the actual slides and notebooks I use, kept public and kept current, so they stay useful long after the room empties.
